Initial release of AutoType X1.

• Bluetooth HID keyboard emulation over Bluetooth Classic (API 28+)
• Human-like typing profiles: Natural, Casual, Professional, Speed Demon
• QWERTY adjacency-based realistic typo simulation and self-correction
• Script management: create, edit, delete, and select typing scripts
• Foreground typing service with persistent notification
• Device discovery and saved device reconnection
• Material 3 UI with light/dark mode support
• Fully offline — no internet, no telemetry, no analytics
